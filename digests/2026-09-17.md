# Digest — 2026-09-17

- **`[ACTION]` Claude Code v2.1.274 (2026-09-16)** — one version ahead of yesterday's v2.1.273. New `CLAUDE_CODE_MCP_STARTUP_WAIT_MS` env var bounds how long the first non-interactive turn waits for MCP servers to finish connecting (`0` = don't wait at all) — useful for headless/scheduled sessions (including this KB routine) that shouldn't stall on a slow-to-start MCP server. New `effort` attribute added to the `claude_code.llm_request` OpenTelemetry trace span (matching `api_request`), for anyone piping Claude Code telemetry into observability tooling. Reliability fixes: sessions no longer loop forever on "unexpected tool_use_id" errors; a critical-memory warning now shows steps to free memory or restart safely instead of just degrading; fixed plugins with a top-level `$schema` key in `hooks.json` wrongly showing an "unknown key" notice. [Docs changelog](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/changelog)
- **`[ACTION]` Catch-up: Claude for Small Business (launched 2026-05-13, never logged in this KB — predates its tracking start)** — a Cowork package built specifically for small businesses/consultancies: 15 ready-to-run agentic workflows, 15 reusable skills, and connectors to QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva, DocuSign, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, and Slack. Workflows cover payroll planning, month-end close, business performance monitoring, and marketing campaign management; skills include cash-flow forecasting, invoice chasing, contract review, lead triage, and content strategy. Users toggle it on in Cowork, connect their tools, and Claude prepares actions for review — sensitive actions (sending, posting, paying) require explicit approval. Surfaced now via a "Claude for Small Business" September training-tour mention, not a new launch. Directly relevant to LanziCo (consulting) and Odoovers Growth given the QuickBooks/HubSpot/DocuSign/Canva/Slack connector overlap with typical small-business ops.
